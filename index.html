A U.S. supplier built around how the government buys.

Blueoak Capital is a New Jersey-based supplier to the U.S. Government. We source industrial and MRO products from qualified U.S. and allied manufacturers, package and label them to military standards, and deliver them to installations and project sites in the Middle East.

Our customers include Defense Logistics Agency supply chains, military installations, and the prime contractors that support them.

  • SAM.gov registered and active on DLA DIBBS
  • Deliveries to CONUS depots and OCONUS sites
  • Country-of-origin and traceability documentation on every order
  • In-region forwarding partners and bonded storage

How we work

A straightforward process that matches how government contracts are awarded, performed and accepted.

1

Quote

We monitor DLA DIBBS and SAM.gov daily and respond to solicitations and direct RFQs with firm pricing, lead time and country of origin.

2

Source

Material is purchased from qualified manufacturers and authorized distributors and screened for Buy American and Trade Agreements Act requirements.

3

Inspect & pack

Each lot is inspected against the specification, packed to MIL-STD-2073 and marked to MIL-STD-129, with a certificate of conformance.

4

Deliver

Forwarding, customs and final delivery, with acceptance through Wide Area Workflow so the order can be closed and paid.
