About us

One accountable partner for everything the mission needs on the ground.

Blueoak Capital LLC is a privately held U.S. small business headquartered in New Jersey with regional coordination in Dubai. We were built to give contracting officers and prime contractors a single counterparty in the Middle East for the material, the construction and the services that keep installations and programs running.

We quote the solicitation as written, source from qualified manufacturers and authorized distributors, self-perform or team on delivery, and close every order with the documentation the receiving activity needs.

How we deliver

Compliance built into every step.The Blueoak Delivery Standard

Government work fails on paperwork more often than on product. Our delivery standard ties every step to the record the next one needs, so acceptance and payment are never held up by a missing document.

From the first quote to the receiving report in Wide Area Workflow, the same team owns the order, and the quote, the packaging and the invoice all say the same thing.

How we deliver
STEP 1

Quote

Firm price, lead time and country of origin against the solicitation as written, within one business day.

STEP 2

Source

Qualified manufacturers and authorized distributors only, with Buy American and TAA screening recorded.

STEP 3

Inspect & pack

Lot inspection, MIL-STD-2073 packaging, MIL-STD-129 marking and a certificate of conformance.

STEP 4

Deliver

Forwarding, customs and final delivery to the installation or site, tracking shared with the COR.

STEP 5

Close

Receiving report and invoice through Wide Area Workflow so the order can be accepted and paid.
