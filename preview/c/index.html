The company

Blueoak Capital is a privately held supplier based in New Jersey. We exist for one kind of buyer: the contracting officer or prime contractor who needs material in the Middle East and needs it accepted on arrival.

We quote the solicitation as written, source from qualified manufacturers and authorized distributors, pack to military standard and file the receiving report ourselves.

Small enough that the person who quoted your solicitation answers the phone. Disciplined enough that the quote, the packaging and the receiving report all say the same thing.
