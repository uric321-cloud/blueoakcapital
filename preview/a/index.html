The firm

Blueoak Capital LLC is a privately held supplier headquartered in New Jersey with regional coordination in Dubai.

We sell to contracting officers and prime contractors who need U.S.-origin and allied-origin material in the Middle East, with the paperwork that lets them accept it.

We quote against the solicitation as written, source from qualified manufacturers and authorized distributors, package to military standard, and deliver to the installation or project site with every document the receiving activity needs to close the order.

How an order moves

Quote to close, without surprises.

Every step produces the record the next one needs, so acceptance and payment are never held up by missing paperwork.

I

Quote

We monitor DLA DIBBS and SAM.gov daily and respond to solicitations and direct RFQs with firm pricing, lead time and country of origin.

II

Source

Material is purchased from qualified manufacturers and authorized distributors and screened for Buy American and Trade Agreements Act requirements.

III

Inspect & pack

Each lot is inspected against the specification, packed to MIL-STD-2073 and marked to MIL-STD-129, with a certificate of conformance.

IV

Ship

Forwarding, customs and final delivery to the installation or project site, with tracking shared with the contracting officer's representative.

V

Close

Receiving report and invoice submitted through Wide Area Workflow so the order can be accepted and paid without follow-up.
