03 — How we work

Built for the way the government buys.

We monitor DLA DIBBS and SAM.gov daily and quote against the solicitation as written: firm price, lead time and country of origin. Material is bought from qualified manufacturers and authorized distributors, inspected, packed to MIL-STD-2073 and marked to MIL-STD-129.

05 — Order flow

Quote to close, without surprises.

Every step produces the record the next one needs, so acceptance and payment are never held up by missing paperwork.

Step 1

Quote

Firm pricing, lead time and country of origin against the solicitation as written. Technical questions raised before the deadline, not after award.

Step 2

Source

Qualified manufacturers and authorized distributors only. Buy American and TAA screening recorded on every line item.

Step 3

Inspect & pack

Lot inspection against the specification, MIL-STD-2073 packaging, MIL-STD-129 marking and a certificate of conformance in every shipment.

Step 4

Deliver & close

Forwarding, customs and final delivery with tracking shared with the COR. Receiving report and invoice filed through Wide Area Workflow.
